# 2. World Report

[Spotify Link](https://open.spotify.com/show/5qaYz2SRxlPUszXZQWNl1U?si=dc618986614b48cd)

I listen to World Report pretty much every day. It allows me to keep a pulse on international and national news without a huge time commitment. If there's a particular story that I'm interested in, then I can follow up. Each episode is ten minutes long and I typically tune in during my drive to work or while I'm getting ready.
