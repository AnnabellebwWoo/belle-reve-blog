Last weekend I decided to go to St. Martin's for some much-needed r&r. In all transparency, I've been disappointed in myself since some bad news earlier in the month. It's been tough coping with the loss and I wanted some space from the local area. I wanted a quiet weekend to myself to reflect and move forward. Below, I chronicle my weekend getaway in St. Martin's.

Late one night I booked an Airbnb and got ready to go. St. Martin's seemed ideal because of the beautiful natural landmark (sea caves!), awesome hiking proximity, and apparently the best lobster roll in New Brunswick. Thursday night I had my bags packed so I was ready to go straight after work. I had a vague plan of places I wanted to hit to take full advantage of my time. 

# Friday 

The three hour drive really blew by with my wicked queue lined up. I had *Summer Driving Mix* from Spotify and *World Report* from CBC bumping. 

![driving selfie](/images/coastal/driving-selfie.JPG)  

On the way to the Airbnb, I stopped at the sea caves. I recommend going in the morning or evening to avoid "crowds". I mean that's kind of an exaggeration, it really doesn't get insanely busy. Even at peak times, it's still quiet enough to relax and read by the water. But I do recommend walking inside the caves at low tide. 

![sea cave](/images/coastal/sea-cave.JPG)  

My accommodation was adorable and perfect for myself. It was conveniently situated between the sea cave/main street and Fundy Trail Provincial Park. The interior was fitted with appropriate coastal decor and the view was killer at sunset.

![bnb view](/images/coastal/bnb-view.JPG)  

I was pretty gassed out after work and the drive so I caught up with my family and some friends then passed out. 

# Saturday

I started by walking along the shore of the sea cave beach to the main street. I got a coffee at [Shipyard Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YxC87UV1pXPCgow29) and the baristas were so sweet! They gave me hiking recs for Fundy Park and so did some local patrons. Specifically, they mentioned Seely beach to Long beach, but be aware of tides. The hike involves some water crossings, so be sure to go at low tide. Macleod is also highly praised and many say the Eye of the Needle is a must. 

![boats](/images/coastal/boats.JPG)  

![cafe selfie](/images/coastal/cafe-selfie.JPG)  

I continued along the main street and visited every single kitschy gift shop. I got a soy candle scented Atlantic Sea Salt + Caramel with embedded sea glass. The highlight is the lighthouse watercolour printed on the side. I also got a short glass with sailboats along the edge, which will be perfect for orange juice in the morning (anemia; iykyk). And of course, loaded up on postcards from local artists. 

![art shop](/images/coastal/art-shop.JPG)  

Every single local I spoke to said [Spinney's](https://maps.app.goo.gl/W39kBWue1yiLGwz16) was the place to go for a lobster roll. After my experience, I have to say, it does live up to the hype. They were efficient, the quality was great, and the price was fair. They apparently have two boats, so their supply is fresh and abundant. 

![lob roll](/images/coastal/lob-roll.JPG)  

I walked the entire length of the main street back to my car then went back to my airbnb to change and pack for a hike. I got to Fundy Trail Provincial Park at around 5PM and they close at 8, so I had to make this quick. The drive itself blew my mind and I knew with only three hours, I could only fit in a brief hike and driving would allow me to see more. I ended up hiking a portion of the Seely to Long Beach hike then spent the rest of the time driving as far as I could. 

![hiking selfie](/images/coastal/hiking-selfie.JPG)  

I capped off the night by reading *Freakonomics* by the beach. I ended up finishing it and I've really been enjoying listening to [Freakonomics Radio](https://open.spotify.com/show/6z4NLXyHPga1UmSJsPK7G1?si=7ecc18b136e2416d) lately. 

# Sunday

I drove to the Quaco Head Lighthouse and there's even a small hike that leads to a beach. I didn't end up doing it but maybe I'll check it out next time. If you're ever visiting St Martin's, this lighthouse is a must-hit. The ocean is bright blue and the rugged rocks guide out to a private, scenic spot. 

![lighthouse](/images/coastal/lighthouse.JPG)  

The farmers market was my next spot but it was pretty lackluster in all honesty. I didn't find anything worth taking home and went back to Shipyard to get another coffee. 

On the way back, I visited Fredericton to return a powder at Sephora and stopped at [Out'Fits](https://maps.app.goo.gl/16bGe8mDkGNRXd8A8), but also didn't find anything good.

--

I thought I would find some inner peace after this weekend. And don't get me wrong, it was beautiful and I'm glad I visited. But I feel a vast emptiness. I'll give myself grace though, because I know this always blows over. I just need to keep my head down and keep working, and in a couple days or weeks I'll feel better.
